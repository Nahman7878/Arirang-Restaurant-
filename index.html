SINCE 2009

To us, "Arirang" means a good person.

In Korea, *Arirang* is a timeless melody, but the word itself carries a deeper, everyday meaning—it talks about a warm-hearted, fundamentally good person.

When we opened back in 2009, we built everything around that simple idea. To us, cooking authentic food isn't just about following old recipes or sourcing premium ingredients. It’s an act of care. It’s the exact same warmth, honesty, and effort you put in when you're welcoming a close friend into your own home.

We’ve been bringing the soul of Korean cooking to Addis Ababa for over a decade now. No shortcuts, no pretension—just real, comforting food made by people who genuinely care. Come pull up a chair, we'd love to take care of you.
